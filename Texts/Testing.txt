The authorities were alerted by her neighbours, who hadn’t seen her in days. May 17th was their last instance of communication. Despite her age, Róisín seems to have been living alone. All databases indicate she doesn’t even exist; the name de Valognes may even be fabricated.

A person who doesn’t exist has just vanished. Suicide is more prominent in these scenarios. Her neighbours insist she was a joyful person. The small following of hers has been investigated, and individuals will face legal consequences for possession of child sexual abuse material. After questioning, nobody seems to have known anything, but all were aware of her age.

Róisín may have been forced to distribute this, or forced to stop distributing it, but not necessarily forced to stop creating it. One thing is certain: Róisín exists.
